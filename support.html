Support

Have a question or need help? Email onceuponafixerupper@gmail.com. We typically respond within 24–48 hours.

Common questions

How do I start a project with Hank?

Tap the chat icon and describe what you want to do. Hank asks a question or two, then gives you the fix or builds the gameplan.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the app, tap your avatar, tap Go Pro, then Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → D.I.Y. DAD Pro → Cancel.

App not working as expected?

Email us with your device model and iOS version.
