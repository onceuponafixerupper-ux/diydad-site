Privacy Policy — D.I.Y. DAD

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Overview

D.I.Y. DAD ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

1. Camera Photos

When you use the camera to scan a home improvement project:

  • The photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI API for analysis
  • Photos are not stored on our servers after analysis
  • Photos are not used to train AI models
  • You can delete the photo from your device anytime

2. Chat Messages

  • Your messages to Hank are sent to Claude AI for coaching responses
  • Message history is stored locally on your device only
  • We do not store or log your conversations on our servers
  • You can clear chat history anytime in Settings

3. Subscription Data

  • When you purchase Pro, Apple processes the payment through App Store
  • We receive only a confirmation that your subscription is active
  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details
  • Apple handles all payment processing and privacy

4. App Usage (Optional)

  • We do not track analytics or user behavior
  • We do not use Firebase, Mixpanel, or other tracking services
  • We do not collect your location, device ID, or other identifiers

5. Saved Projects

  • Projects you save are stored locally on your device only
  • They are not synced to cloud servers
  • Only you can access your saved projects

How We Use Your Information

  • Photos: Analyzed by Claude AI to generate DIY gameplans. Immediately discarded after analysis.
  • Messages: Processed by Claude AI to provide coaching. Not stored by us.
  • Subscription: Managed by Apple. Used only to unlock Pro features.

Third Parties

Claude AI (Anthropic)

  • Photos and messages are sent to Anthropic's API for processing
  • [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)

Apple App Store

  • Handles all subscription payments and billing
  • [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/)

Affiliate Links

  • D.I.Y. DAD is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com
  • As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases
  • When you order parts via Amazon, Home Depot, or Lowe's links, those retailers collect data per their own privacy policies
  • We earn a small affiliate commission but do not receive your order data

Your Rights

You can:

  • Stop using the App anytime
  • Delete saved projects from your device
  • Cancel your Pro subscription via Apple Settings
  • Request data deletion (since we don't store data on servers, there's nothing to delete)

Contact

If you have privacy questions, email: onceuponafixerupper@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy anytime. Changes take effect when posted to the App.

Bottom line: We don't store your photos or messages. Hank helps you DIY. That's it.
